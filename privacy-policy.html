Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 6, 2026

RRB ACE ("the App") is an offline exam preparation app for the Indian Railway Recruitment Board NTPC CBT-1 exam. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All quiz data, progress, bookmarks, and exam results are stored locally on your device using an offline database. We do not have access to this data.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect certain data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This may include device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's Advertising Policies.

Data Storage

All user data (quiz scores, bookmarks, study progress) is stored exclusively on your device. No data is uploaded to any server. If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for exam preparation and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Internet — required solely for loading advertisements.
  • Network State — to check connectivity before loading ads.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
