Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

RRB ACE ("the App") is a free practice app for Indian Railway recruitment exams (RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician, JE and RPF). This Privacy Policy describes what information the App handles, who receives it, and the choices you have.

Your Study Data Stays on Your Device

Your answers, scores, bookmarks, unfinished mock exams, daily streak, chosen exam, reminder time, language preference and the time until which the full question bank is unlocked are stored on your device. The App has no accounts and we do not operate a server that receives this data.

If backup is switched on in your Android settings, Android may include the App's data in the backup it keeps in your own Google account, so that it can be restored on a new phone. We cannot access that backup.

Usage Statistics

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is used, so we can improve it. This records events such as the App being opened, a practice set or mock exam being started or finished, the score of a mock exam, and the language chosen. Along with these events Google receives:

— An app-instance identifier and, where available, the Advertising ID
— Device model, Android version and App version
— Approximate location at country or region level, derived from the IP address

If you use "Report a problem" on a question, the number of that question and the reason you picked are sent in the same way, so that we can correct it.

We do not record the text of questions you answer, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Crash Reports

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics. If the App crashes, a report is sent to Google containing the technical details of the crash, the device model and Android version, and an installation identifier. We use these reports only to find and fix faults.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including optional video advertisements that you can choose to watch to unlock the full question bank for a day. Watching them is never required: about 2,000 questions are always available. AdMob may collect information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

— Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
— IP address
— General location (country/region level)
— App usage data for ad relevance

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's Settings > Google > Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time through "Ad privacy settings" at the bottom of the App's home screen.

Third-Party Services

The services above are provided by Google. Google's use of the data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Permissions

— Internet and network state: to load advertisements and to send usage statistics and crash reports. Practising questions works without a connection.
— Notifications (optional): only if you switch on the daily study reminder. You can switch it off in the App's settings or in Android settings at any time.
— Run at startup: so that the daily reminder, if you switched it on, still works after the phone restarts.
— Advertising ID: used by Google's advertising and analytics services as described above.

Keeping and Deleting Data

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes everything stored on your device. Usage statistics and crash reports are kept by Google for a limited period and are not linked to your name. You can reset your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. To ask about data relating to your installation, contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
